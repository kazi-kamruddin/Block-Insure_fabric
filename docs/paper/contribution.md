# Thesis contribution and evidence

Block-Insure evaluates whether evidence provenance changes medical claim routing when Oracle workers agree on the same computation. Each request pins the issuer keys and feed roots. Authority is scoped per fact. The worker checks signed records and their origin and derivation links, evaluates a deterministic fact vector, and commits to that vector and its policy decision. Two of three distinct subjects must reveal the same result. The [offline Phase 3 experiment](../results/oracle-v2-phase3-2026-11-25.md) pairs the same synthetic cases across the four routing modes and sweeps panel capacity.

The insurer, hospital, claimant and bank roles represent different evidence scopes in the prototype. A contradiction, unavailable proof, lineage dependency, model referral, or missing Oracle quorum can send the case to a governed review. Approval binds the current claim version, episode, issued payout terms and relevant decision evidence to a certificate. The bank checks the effective certificate before simulated BDT settlement. R2 exercised sealed human ballots and a transcript-bound v3 certificate. [R1 journeys](../results/r1-journeys-2026-11-25.md) · [R2 journeys](../results/r2-journeys-2026-11-26.md)

## Negative results

Workers can agree on a false fact when they share a source. In the offline synthetic lineage case all three simulated workers agreed with the signed copies before the provenance gate checked their common origin. The experiment included a Hospital payment confirmation derived from its own invoice and a separately signed synthetic Bank contradiction. Separate issuer keys do not by themselves establish independent evidence. Consistent in-band Hospital and claimant collusion remained possible where the configured facts contained no independent amount observation. [Dated offline experiment](../results/oracle-v2-phase3-2026-11-25.md)

An earlier commit design admitted an identical commitment copied from another Oracle. The revised commit preimage binds channel, request, claim version and Oracle subject, and chaincode refuses identical offers. Quorum requires two valid openings from distinct assigned subjects. The offline synthetic workload separates wrong automatic approvals, legitimate referrals, unresolved overflow and liveness outcomes. Its aggregate rates depend on the scenario mixture. [R1 live protocol](../results/r1-journeys-2026-11-25.md) · [Dated offline experiment](../results/oracle-v2-phase3-2026-11-25.md)

A second copy path appeared at the pre-freeze audit. Binding the preimage stops a worker reusing another worker's commitment, but it does not stop a worker choosing its value after another worker's reveal. Fabric accepts a proposal timestamp up to 15 s behind the peer clock, and up to sequence 3 the commit gate compared only that timestamp with the deadline. A worker that withheld its commitment could therefore commit a copied fact vector after the first reveal, turning an honest 1-to-1 disagreement into a false 2-of-3. The same shape affected sealed panel ballots. Sequence 4 closes sealing in state: the first reveal ends commitment for that request or review, and MVCC serialises a racing pair. Three Go tests reproduce the backdated copy; they failed on the sequence-3 source and pass on sequence 4. The lesson for permissioned Oracle designs is that deadline checks on client-chosen timestamps need a state transition behind them, because the clock tolerance is a window an adversary can choose to use.
